## 📌 Project Overview

This project is **Day 11 of my 30 Days, 30 Dashboards Challenge**, where I am building one data analytics dashboard every day using different business datasets and analytical approaches.

For Day 11, I created an interactive **Financial Performance Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI**.

The objective of this dashboard is to provide a clear and interactive view of the organization's financial performance by analyzing important financial metrics such as:

- Revenue
- Expenses
- Profit
- Profitability
- Financial Trends
- Business Segment Performance
- Cost Patterns
- Growth Trends

The dashboard transforms raw financial data into meaningful visual insights that can help business stakeholders understand financial performance and make data-driven decisions.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Financial data can become difficult to understand when it is stored across large tables and multiple records.

Business stakeholders need answers to questions such as:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- How is profit changing over time?
- Which areas are generating the highest revenue?
- Where are expenses increasing?
- Which segments are the most profitable?
- Are costs growing faster than revenue?
- What areas require cost optimization?
- What are the major financial trends?

The purpose of this dashboard is to answer these questions through an easy-to-use interactive Power BI report.

# 🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify several important financial insights.

### 1. Revenue Trends

Analyzing revenue over time helps identify periods of:

- Growth
- Decline
- Stability
- Seasonal changes

Understanding these trends can help businesses plan future strategies.

---

### 2. Profitability

Revenue alone does not indicate strong financial performance.

By comparing revenue with expenses, the dashboard helps determine which areas are actually generating higher profits.

---

### 3. Expense Optimization

Tracking expenses over time can reveal areas where costs are increasing.

These areas can then be investigated for possible cost optimization.

---

### 4. Segment Contribution

Different business segments may contribute differently to revenue and profit.

Comparing segments helps identify the strongest contributors to overall financial performance.

---

### 5. Financial Performance Monitoring

The dashboard provides a centralized view of important financial KPIs, making it easier to monitor business performance without manually analyzing large datasets.

# 🧹 Data Preparation

Before building the dashboard, the dataset was prepared for analysis.

The data preparation process included:

1. Importing the financial dataset.
2. Reviewing the available columns.
3. Checking for missing values.
4. Identifying duplicate records.
5. Correcting data types.
6. Cleaning unnecessary fields.
7. Standardizing values.
8. Creating required calculated columns.
9. Loading the cleaned data into Power BI.
