# Day 7 - Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition
and identify workforce factors associated with employee turnover.

## 🎯 Business Problem
Organizations need to understand why employees leave and which departments,
job roles, salary groups, age groups, and workplace factors are associated
with higher attrition.

## 💡 Key Insights
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Attrition Count: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Analyzed Attrition by Department
- Analyzed Attrition by Job Role
- Compared Overtime vs Attrition
- Analyzed Attrition by Age Group
- Compared Salary Groups
- Studied Job Satisfaction vs Attrition

## 🚀 Business Action
The insights can help HR teams identify high-risk employee groups,
improve workload management, review compensation, and develop
employee-retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Visualization
