
# Day 5 - Superstore Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Superstore sales,
profit, orders, customers, products, and regional performance.

## 🎯 Business Problem
Businesses need to understand sales and profitability trends to identify
high-performing products, categories, regions, and areas requiring improvement.

## 💡 Key Insights
- Analyzed overall Sales, Profit, Orders and Quantity
- Tracked monthly sales and profit trends
- Compared regional performance
- Identified top-performing categories and sub-categories
- Analyzed customer and segment performance
- Identified profitable and underperforming products

## 🚀 Business Action
These insights can help businesses optimize product strategy,
improve regional performance, focus on profitable segments, and reduce losses.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization
