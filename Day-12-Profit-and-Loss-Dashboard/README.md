# 📊 Day 12 - Profit & Loss Dashboard




## 📌 Project Overview

This project is **Day 12 of my 30 Days, 30 Dashboards Challenge**.

For Day 12, I created an interactive **Profit & Loss Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze the financial performance of companies across different industries and business segments.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of important financial metrics including **Total Sales, Operating Profit, Net Profit, Operating Margin, Net Profit Margin, and Growth Performance**.

The objective is to transform financial data into meaningful business insights that can support better financial decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Financial performance data can be difficult to analyze when it is presented in large tables or spreadsheets.

Business stakeholders need a simple way to understand:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- How much operating profit is being earned?
- What is the net profit?
- Which companies are performing better?
- How profitable are different companies?
- How have sales and profits changed over time?
- How is the latest quarter performing?

This dashboard provides an interactive solution to answer these questions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are:

1. Analyze total sales performance.
2. Track operating profit and net profit.
3. Analyze operating and net profit margins.
4. Compare sales with operating profit.
5. Compare revenue with net profit.
6. Compare profitability across companies.
7. Analyze 3-year sales and profit growth.
8. Evaluate latest quarter performance.
9. Provide actionable financial insights.

# 📈 Dashboard Analysis

## 1. Sales vs Operating Profit

This analysis compares the total sales generated by different companies with their operating profit.

It helps identify companies that are generating higher sales as well as companies that are converting sales into operating profit effectively.

### Business Questions

- Which companies have the highest sales?
- Which companies generate higher operating profit?
- Is higher sales associated with higher operating profit?

---

## 2. Revenue vs Net Profit

The dashboard compares revenue with net profit across companies.

This helps understand how much profit remains after considering the overall costs and expenses of the business.

### Key Benefits

- Identify profitable companies.
- Compare revenue contribution.
- Understand profit generation.
- Evaluate financial efficiency.

---

## 3. Profitability Comparison

A profitability comparison is presented using an interactive visualization.

This makes it easier to compare the contribution of operating profit and net profit across the analyzed companies.

The analysis can help stakeholders identify companies with stronger profitability performance.

---

## 4. 3-Year Sales vs Profit Growth

The dashboard analyzes historical growth using:

- Average Sales Growth
- Average Profit Growth

This helps identify companies with stronger long-term growth performance.

### Business Questions

- Which companies have higher sales growth?
- Which companies have stronger profit growth?
- Are sales and profit growing at a similar rate?
- Which companies show stronger long-term performance?

---

## 5. Latest Quarter P&L Performance

The dashboard also provides an overview of the latest quarter's:

- Sales
- Profit

This helps compare recent financial performance and identify companies currently performing strongly.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can consider:

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Companies

Companies showing strong sales and profitability can be studied to understand the factors driving their performance.

### 2. Monitor Profitability

High sales do not always mean high profitability. Businesses should monitor both revenue and profit.

### 3. Control Costs

Companies with strong revenue but relatively weaker profit performance should investigate their cost structure.

### 4. Track Growth

Long-term sales and profit growth should be monitored to identify sustainable business performance.

### 5. Monitor Quarterly Performance

Regular quarterly analysis can help businesses identify changes in financial performance and take corrective action quickly.
