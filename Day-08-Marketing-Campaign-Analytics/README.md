# Day 8 - Marketing Campaign Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze marketing campaign
performance, customer engagement, conversions, and campaign effectiveness.

## 🎯 Business Problem
Marketing teams need to understand which campaigns, channels, and customer
segments generate the best engagement and conversions while optimizing
marketing spend.

## 💡 Key Insights
- Analyzed overall campaign performance
- Compared campaign response and conversion rates
- Evaluated different marketing channels
- Analyzed customer segments and demographics
- Identified high-performing campaigns
- Compared campaign effectiveness and engagement

## 🚀 Business Action
These insights can help marketing teams identify the most effective
campaigns and channels, improve customer targeting, optimize marketing
spending, and increase conversion rates.
